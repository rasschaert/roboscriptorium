# Golden books: notes per pair

What each golden pair is (edition, scan, reference), what makes it hard, and what is
known to deviate between its print and its reference. The numbers (set, pages, layer
CER, unplaced lines) are generated into [golden-books.md](golden-books.md); the rules
for choosing, checking and retiring a pair are in AGENTS.md.

Retired (files deleted, manifests in git history): Boze tongen, De dode
kamer, Sense and Sensibility (all three scans), Goede dochter's eighth printing, and
(2026-10-10) Lady into Fox and Grand Hotel Europa, whose references don't match their
prints; (2026-10-13, the user) 11/22/63 (6.7% of its body lines unplaced), FKA USA (30%,
its footnotes among them) and Koning Wilders (its text layer at 2.45% CER, over three
times the set's median), the suspect pairs whose scores measured their references more
than the pipeline.
