## Faithfulness

How closely each reference follows its print, most faithful first. A faithful pair
is the arbiter of every change: its reference is what the build must turn the scan into.
*Bare-word* is the text layer against the reference, punctuation aside: OCR slips only,
at the layer's own rate, where the two are the same text. *Edition verdicts* are places a
human found the reference itself departing from the print (per 100 scored pages);
*declared* are the departures its manifest undoes. Two cautions: bare-word mixes the
scan's OCR slips with the reference's departures (a clean scan of a faithful pair scores
lowest), and no edition verdicts can mean no review yet, not faithfulness. A born-digital
PDF (calibre's) scores 0 by construction and is out of scope.
